A camera-first room editor that turns 6–12 photos into editable 2D and 3D plans. A deterministic layout engine applies 12 spatial principles and five Neufert clearances to generate fit-verified options, while a parallel recommendation pipeline ranks products across eight retailers. Unverified dimensions are excluded from placement.
